How I work

Four things I'll argue about.

01

Fix the template, not the page.

One page is an afternoon. The template behind it is thousands of pages and it compounds. I look for the change that lands everywhere before I look for the change that looks impressive.

02

Rendering is a business decision.

If content only exists after JavaScript runs, it doesn't exist for a crawler — and increasingly not for an AI answer either. This is the least glamorous conversation in SEO and the one that decides everything downstream.

03

Scale amplifies what's already there.

Programmatic pages multiply your quality or multiply your problems in exactly equal measure. I won't build the machine until the thing it's replicating is worth replicating.

04

A number without a baseline is a claim.

Every result on this site names its comparison window. "+300%" off a base of four is a rounding error wearing a suit, and anyone senior will ask.
